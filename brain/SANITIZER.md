# SANITIZER: what never goes in the brain

Anyone in the brain can read any page, including the people and brands it's about. Write every page as if they are reading it.

## Never goes in

1. **Opinions about people.** "Flaky," "difficult," "checked out." Write what happened, with a date, or leave it out.
2. **Anything said in private.** Pasted DMs, side comments after a client drops off a call, "between us." These stay out even when they're useful.
3. **Commercial terms.** Our fees, margins, rates.
4. **Personal data.** Phone numbers, home addresses, health, family.
5. **Raw transcripts.** Recaps only. The transcript stays in the recording tool.

## The check every write passes

Before saving any page, ask:
- Would the person or brand named here be fine reading this line?
- Is every performance number (GMV, ad spend, active affiliates, retention) linked to the sheet rather than typed in?
- Did anything come from a DM or a side comment?

One "no" on the first two, or one "yes" on the third, and the line doesn't go in. If it matters, put a note in the recap's "Flags for a person" section saying something was held back, without repeating it.

`brain/knowledge/reference/accounts-sheet.csv` is a source-of-truth export ("consult, don't edit", RESOLVER). I am not proposing any change to it. The sheet owner should decide, after checking the points below.

**What I would ask the sheet owner to verify before any GMV change:**

1. **Source system.** What feeds `gmv_last_30d_usd` (which export, from which platform), and when was it last pulled? Which system did Kettle & Crumb's finance team use?

3. **Window.** What are the exact start and end dates of each 30-day window? The sheet is as of 2026-09-21. Dana's "last thirty days" was said on 2026-09-24, but finance's end date was not stated.
4. **Definition.** For both figures:
   - gross or net of refunds, returns and cancellations;
   - whether seller-funded discounts, shipping and tax are included;
   - whether GMV is counted by order date or by settlement date.
5. **Currency.** The sheet is USD. Confirm finance's figure is too.
6. **Whether to change anything at all.** If the two figures measure different things, keep the sheet column as it is. Record finance's figure as a separate, defined metric instead of overwriting.

**Why the call's figure may not be comparable:**

- It comes from the client's finance team, not from the export behind the sheet.
- Its channel scope was asked and not answered.
- Its 30-day window ends on a different, unstated date.
- Its definition (gross or net, what is included) is unknown.
- A three-day shift in the window is unlikely to explain the size of the gap on its own. That points to a difference in scope or definition, not just timing.
